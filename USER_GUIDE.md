# NeoTune User & Operator Manual

Welcome to **NeoTune**, your all-in-one cross-platform streaming audio hub. This guide covers all user features, keyboard shortcuts, voice commands, and multi-device modes.

---

## 1. Exploring & Playing Radio Stations

- **Search Anything**: Click the global search bar or press <kbd>/</kbd> to search 50,000+ stations by name (e.g. *"BBC"*, *"KEXP"*), music genre (e.g. *"Synthwave"*, *"Jazz"*, *"Lo-Fi"*), or country (e.g. *"Japan"*, *"Vietnam"*).
- **Genre Pills**: Click on genre chips (Ambient, Top 40, Electronic, Classical, etc.) to immediately filter stations.
- **Country Picker**: Tap the country globe button to browse stations from over 100+ countries with national flags and station counts.
- **Favorites & Pinning**: Tap the ❤️ heart button on any station tile to save it to your Favorites.
- **Custom Station URLs**: Click the **"+ Add Station"** button in Favorites to add custom Icecast, Shoutcast, or HLS `.m3u8` stream links.

---

## 2. Podcast Directory & Player

- **Discover Shows**: Switch to the **Podcasts** tab to search millions of shows via Apple iTunes Podcasts.
- **Episode Guide**: Tap any podcast show card to slide out the episode drawer.
- **Playback Controls**: Seek backward 15s (<kbd>J</kbd>) or forward 30s (<kbd>L</kbd>), adjust speed (0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x), and pick up where you left off.

---

## 3. Immersive Studio Player & Visualizers

Click on the **Mini Player** at the bottom of the screen to open the **Full Player Studio**.

- **Switch Visualizer Skins**: Click the visualizer button to cycle through:
  - 📊 *Frequency Bars*
  - 〰️ *Oscilloscope*
  - ⭕ *Circular Radial*
  - 🌈 *Rainbow Waves*
  - 🎗️ *Neon Ribbons*
  - 👾 *Cyberpunk Matrix*
- **Record Live Audio**: Click the ⏺️ **Record** button in the studio player to record the live broadcast. Click stop to download the `.webm` audio file.
- **Live ICY Metadata**: View currently playing artist and song title in real time with quick Google/YouTube search buttons.

---

## 4. 10-Band Equalizer & Sound Customization

Open the **Equalizer** (<kbd>E</kbd>) to fine-tune your acoustics:
- **10 Frequencies**: 32Hz, 64Hz, 125Hz, 250Hz, 500Hz, 1kHz, 2kHz, 4kHz, 8kHz, 16kHz.
- **Bass Boost**: Toggle the sub-bass harmonic booster.
- **Spatial Audio**: Select between *Direct*, *Stage Widener*, *Studio Room*, or *Concert Hall*.
- **Presets**: Flat, Bass Boost, Vocal, Club, Jazz, Rock.

---

## 5. Specialized Device Modes

### 🚗 Car Mode (<kbd>C</kbd>)
- Minimalist, high-contrast user interface designed for driving safety.
- Extra-large touch buttons for Play/Pause, Next Station, and Volume.
- Quick 1-tap grid of your favorite preset stations.

### 📺 Smart TV 10-Foot Interface (<kbd>T</kbd>)
- Designed for living room Smart TVs and projectors.
- Full D-Pad remote control support: navigate with Up/Down/Left/Right arrows and press Enter/OK to select.
- High-visibility glowing focus rings.

### 🌙 OLED Screensaver (<kbd>S</kbd>)
- Pure black OLED background preventing screen burn-in.
- Subtle drifting digital clock, current station artwork, and live waveform.

---

## 6. Radio Alarm Clock & Sleep Timer

### ⏰ Radio Alarm Clock
- Set custom wake-up alarms with automatic live station playback.
- **AI Weather & Morning Briefing**: Synthesizes local weather and morning greetings when the alarm sounds.
- Customizable snooze intervals (5, 10, 15 minutes).

### ⏳ Sleep Timer
- Set a timer for 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes.
- Audio volume gradually fades out over the last 60 seconds before stopping smoothly.

## 7. Voice Control Commands

Click the 🎙️ **Microphone** icon in the navigation bar and speak naturally:

| Voice Command | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"Play"* / *"Resume"* | Starts audio playback |
| *"Pause"* / *"Stop"* | Pauses audio playback |
| *"Next station"* | Skips to the next station in the list |
| *"Previous station"* | Skips to the previous station |
| *"Volume up"* / *"Volume down"* | Adjusts master volume by 10% |
| *"Mute"* / *"Unmute"* | Toggles audio mute |
| *"Play Jazz"* / *"Play Rock"* | Searches and starts playing the specified genre |
| *"Car mode"* | Activates Car Mode interface |
| *"Open Equalizer"* | Opens the 10-band Equalizer dialog |

---

## 8. Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd> | Play / Pause |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Mute / Unmute |
| <kbd>Arrow Up</kbd> | Increase volume (+5%) |
| <kbd>Arrow Down</kbd> | Decrease volume (-5%) |
| <kbd>Arrow Right</kbd> | Next station |
| <kbd>Arrow Left</kbd> | Previous station |
| <kbd>/</kbd> | Focus global search bar |
| <kbd>E</kbd> | Open 10-Band Equalizer |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | Toggle Car Mode |
| <kbd>T</kbd> | Toggle TV Mode |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | Toggle Screensaver |
| <kbd>F</kbd> | Toggle Favorite for current station |
| <kbd>?</kbd> | Show Keyboard Shortcuts Cheat Sheet |

---

## 9. Multi-Language Support (50 Languages)

NeoTune supports 50 languages. To switch languages:
1. Tap the 🌐 flag icon in the sidebar or header.
2. Search or select your preferred language (e.g. *Tiếng Việt*, *Español*, *Français*, *Deutsch*, *日本語*, *العربية*).
3. The interface immediately updates with native translations and proper text direction (RTL for Arabic, Hebrew, Urdu).

## 10. Cross-Device Cloud Sync

Sign in with your Google account or email via **Firebase Authentication** to:
- Synchronize your Favorite stations across phone, laptop, and tablet.
- Handoff active listening sessions seamlessly between devices.
- Back up custom added radio stream URLs and equalizer presets.
